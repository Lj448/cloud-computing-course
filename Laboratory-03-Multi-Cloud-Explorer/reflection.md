Mission Reflection In this laboratory, I learned about three cloud platforms. These are AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. The cloud platform that impressed me the most is AWS because it has many services that can be used by different companies. I also learned that the three cloud platforms have different features and services. Before doing this activity, I only knew a little about cloud platforms. After doing the research, I learned more about what they can do and how they can help different businesses.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many things that are the same. They all have services for computing, storage, networking, databases, and security. They also allow users to use cloud resources through the internet. The names of their services are different, but they can do similar jobs. Because of this activity, I was able to compare the services of the three cloud platforms and understand their differences better.

I learned that business needs are important when choosing a cloud platform. A company should think about its budget, type of business, current technology, security, and future plans. For example, if a company is already using Microsoft products, Azure can be a good choice. If a company needs AI and machine learning, GCP can also be a good choice. This means that the best cloud platform depends on the needs of the company.

When I acted as a Cloud Solutions Consultant, I learned that choosing a cloud platform is not just about choosing the most popular one. I need to look at what the client needs first. After that, I can choose the platform that can help the client better. I learned that different clients can need different cloud platforms.

My GitHub portfolio also improved because I added different files for this laboratory. I added the AWS, Azure, and GCP research, comparison, recommendations, screenshots, and reflection. I also learned how to organize files in GitHub and use Markdown.

this activity helped me understand cloud computing better. I learned that AWS, Azure, and GCP are useful platforms, but the best choice depends on what the company needs.
